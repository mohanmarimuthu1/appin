A Simple 90-Day Learning Roadmap

Whether you study at home, online or in a classroom, a clear plan keeps you moving. Here is a realistic three-month path for a complete beginner.

Month 1: foundations

Month 2: hands-on campaigns

Month 3: portfolio and job prep

Common Mistakes Beginners Make

Most learners who stall do not lack talent; they fall into a few predictable traps. Avoiding these will save you months.

Questions like “learn digital marketing with google” come down to the same thing: practical skills plus a portfolio.

  • Collecting certificates without running a single real campaign
  • Trying to master every platform at once instead of going deep on two or three
